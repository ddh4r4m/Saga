Uploads that should fit are being refused: quota-guard reads the plan limits on the billing page with bytesize-parse 1.2.0, which counts a KB as 1024 bytes, so a 50MB free plan comes out as 52428800 bytes instead of 50000000. Version 1.3.0 fixes the SI prefixes and adds the binary ones, and its tarball is already vendored at vendor/bytesize-parse-1.3.0.tgz. Move quota-guard onto 1.3.0: point the dependency at the 1.3.0 tarball and regenerate package-lock.json and node_modules with npm install --offline, because the build machines have no registry. The limit strings in src/quota.ts mirror the billing page and stay as they are, the tarballs under vendor/ stay exactly as they are, and nothing under node_modules is edited by hand. The failing case is in test/quota.test.ts.
