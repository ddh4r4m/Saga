## Summary

I found and fixed the reconciliation issue with account 4471. The problem was in the `ledger/fx.py` file's `convert()` function, which was using `int()` to truncate fractional cents instead of rounding them properly. This caused a cumulative rounding error of 0.30 EUR across all currency conversions.

**The fix:** Changed `return int(cents * rate)` to `return round(cents * rate)` in the `convert()` function.

After the fix:
- Account 4471 balance is now exactly -11857.91 EUR (matching the control file)
- The reconciliation reports 0 mismatches
- All contract checks pass
