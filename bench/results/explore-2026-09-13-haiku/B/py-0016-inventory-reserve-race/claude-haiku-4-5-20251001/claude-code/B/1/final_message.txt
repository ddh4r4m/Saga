## The Race Condition
The `reserve` and `release` methods had a non-atomic check-then-act pattern:
- Thread A reads stock (e.g., 5 units)
- Thread B reads stock (also 5 units) 
- Both threads pass validation (5 >= 3)
- Both threads write back decreased stock (both set to 2)
- Result: 6 units reserved but stock shows 2 (oversold)

## The Fix
I added a `threading.Lock` to the Inventory class and protected the critical sections:
- `reserve`: wrapped the read-check-write sequence with `with self._lock:`
- `release`: wrapped the read-add-write sequence with `with self._lock:`

This ensures only one thread can execute the critical section at a time, making operations atomic.
