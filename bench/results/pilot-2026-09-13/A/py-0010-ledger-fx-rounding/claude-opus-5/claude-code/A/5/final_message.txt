**Cause:** `ledger/fx.py:convert` did `int(cents * rate)`, which truncates toward zero. **Fix:** `convert` now computes the product with `Decimal` (using `Decimal(str(rate))` to avoid binary float artifacts) and quantizes half-up to the nearest minor unit.
