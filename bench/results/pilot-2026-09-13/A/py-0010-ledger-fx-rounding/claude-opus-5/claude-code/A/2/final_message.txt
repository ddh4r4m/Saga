**Cause:** `ledger/fx.py:convert` did `int(cents * rate)`, which truncates toward zero on every conversion. Each dropped fraction biases the leg toward zero, and since debits and credits are unbalanced in count, the residue doesn't cancel: it accumulated to exactly -30 cents. Every other account converts at rate 1.0, where truncation is a no-op, which is why they all matched.

**Fix:** `convert` now computes the product as an exact `Decimal` (using `Decimal(str(rate))` so 0.9228 is a true 0.9228, not its binary float neighbour) and quantizes with `ROUND_HALF_EVEN`. This also makes negative amounts round symmetrically, which `int()` did not.
