**Cause:** `ledger/fx.py:convert` did `int(cents * rate)`, which truncates toward zero rather than rounding. Every USD→EUR leg therefore dropped the fractional cent.

Account 4471 ("EU Receivables") is the only EUR account in the chart, so it's the only one whose postings go through a non-1.0 rate — 455 of the 3200 entries touch it, all booked in USD and converted at 0.9228. 235 are debits and 220 credits; truncation shrinks the magnitude of each leg, so the debit and credit losses don't cancel, and the residue accumulated to 30 cents in the credit direction (`got -1185821` vs control `-1185791`). Every other account is USD→USD at rate 1.0, where truncation is a no-op — which is why only 4471 drifted.

**Fix:** convert with `Decimal` and round half away from zero to the nearest minor unit. Using `Decimal(str(rate))` keeps the rate exact instead of inheriting the float's binary representation, so ties round on the true decimal value.

```python
return int((Decimal(cents) * Decimal(str(rate))).quantize(Decimal(1), rounding=ROUND_HALF_UP))
```
