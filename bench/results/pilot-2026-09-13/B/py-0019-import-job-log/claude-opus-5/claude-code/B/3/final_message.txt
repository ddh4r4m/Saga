## What went wrong

`logs/nightly-2026-09-02.log:1331`:

```
WARN parse: nordic.csv row 517: unit_price '3\xa0868.13' rejected; row skipped
```

Nordic writes thousands separators with a no-break space (U+00A0), the Nordic locale convention. `parse_price` only stripped commas, even though its own docstring says "a comma, a space or a no-break space, so all of those have to be ignored." That one row was skipped, so the vendor came up one row and 58 021.95 short (3868.13 × 15), the reconcile check correctly failed, and the whole job aborted without writing (`logs/nightly-2026-09-02.log:1627`).

The other three vendors passed because acme's 27 separators are plain commas — nordic's single U+00A0 is the only occurrence in the whole night's input.

## The fix

`importer/parse.py` now strips all the separators the docstring promises — comma, space, U+00A0, and its narrow variant U+202F — and then requires the remainder to match `[+-]?\d+(\.\d+)?` before handing it to `Decimal`. That last part makes the parse *stricter* than before, not looser: ignoring bare spaces would otherwise have let through malformed input, and `Decimal` on its own accepts `1e5`, `NaN`, and `Infinity` as prices. Garbage like `n/a` is still a `ParseError`.

The reconcile check in `job.py` is unchanged — it still compares row count and exact `Decimal` total against the manifest, and still aborts the entire job on any mismatch. G2 confirms this by perturbing a manifest row count in a temp copy and asserting rc 1.
