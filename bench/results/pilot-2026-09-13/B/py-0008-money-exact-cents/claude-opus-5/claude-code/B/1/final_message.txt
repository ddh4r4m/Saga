## What changed

`Money` now holds an integer `cents` field instead of a float `amount`:

- **`pricing/money.py:1`** — frozen dataclass with `cents: int`. The public constructor still takes dollars (`Money(0.10)`) and rounds once to cents at the boundary; `Money.from_cents()` builds from exact cents and is what the arithmetic operators use, so `+`, `*` and unary `-` are pure integer maths and can't drift. Added `parts()` → `(sign, dollars, cents)` so renderers never divide by 100.
- **`pricing/format.py:1`** — formats from `parts()`: `f"{sign}${dollars:,}.{cents:02d}"`. Output identical (`$1,234.50`, `-$0.05`, `$0.00`, `-$1,234,567.89`).
- **`pricing/tax.py:1`** — `round(subtotal.cents * (1 + rate))`, same half-even rounding at the same cent granularity as the old `round(dollars, 2)`.
- **`pricing/report.py:1`** — same `name,qty,total` header and same plain-decimal column, rendered from the integer parts: `widget,3,0.30` instead of `widget,3,0.30000000000000004`.

`.amount` is gone rather than kept as a float property, since a second float view would reopen the drift path; no caller in the repo referenced it after the edits.
