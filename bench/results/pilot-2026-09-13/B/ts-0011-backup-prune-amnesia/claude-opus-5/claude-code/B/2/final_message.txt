## The fix

Two lines in `src/prune.ts:41-45`:

```ts
// Weekly retention counts the newest keepWeekly weeks, the current week included.
const weeks = newestPerBucket(sorted, (x) => isoWeek(x.takenAt));
for (const b of weeks.slice(0, policy.keepWeekly)) keep.add(b.id);
// Snapshots tagged pinned are legal holds and are never deleted, whatever the policy says.
return sorted.filter((b) => !keep.has(b.id) && !b.tags.includes("pinned")).map((b) => b.id);
```

- **Weekly rule:** `slice(1, keepWeekly)` → `slice(0, keepWeekly)`. That was the 1.7 regression: it both skipped the current week *and* shortened the window by one (`slice(1, 4)` yields 3 weeks). The straight slice restores 1.6 behaviour — `keepWeekly: 8` now really keeps 8 weeks. The stale comment claiming the dailies cover the current week is replaced.
- **Legal hold:** pinned snapshots are filtered out of the returned ids at the end, so they can never be deleted regardless of policy or age. Filtering the output rather than adding to `keep` means it applies uniformly and doesn't consume a daily or weekly slot.
