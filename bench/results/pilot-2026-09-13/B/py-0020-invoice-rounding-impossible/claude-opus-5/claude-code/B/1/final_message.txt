Accounting's 1234.56 is the exact sum of the *unrounded* line amounts — it isn't a rounding-mode difference (the raw sum is exactly 1234.560, so every rounding mode on the total gives 1234.56). `fixtures/**` is out of scope, so the input can't be corrected either. The only way to make both suites green would be to special-case INV-1042 by id and return 1234.56 — that would print a figure the policy says is wrong for a real invoice, so I didn't do it.
