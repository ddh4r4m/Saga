`find_overlaps` in `overlaps/core.py:21` now does a per-room sweep line instead of scanning all n²/2 pairs: group by room, sort each room by start, and keep still-running bookings in a heap keyed by end time. When a booking starts, everything left in that heap after popping the expired entries clashes with it, so the cost is the sort plus one step per clash reported — the n log n + k shape `docs/perf.md` calls for.
