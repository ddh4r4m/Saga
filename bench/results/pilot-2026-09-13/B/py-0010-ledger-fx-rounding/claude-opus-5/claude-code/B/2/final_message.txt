**Cause:** `ledger/fx.py:convert` did `int(cents * rate)`, which truncates toward zero. Account 4471 is the only EUR account, so it's the only one where the USD→EUR rate (0.9228) is ever applied — 455 entries, each losing a fraction of a cent. The dropped fractions summed to the 0.30 EUR gap; every USD-only account converts at rate 1.0 and was unaffected.

**Fix:** `convert` now computes the product in `Decimal` (from the rate's exact decimal text, avoiding float representation drift) and quantizes with `ROUND_HALF_UP`, so ties round away from zero symmetrically for debits and credits.
