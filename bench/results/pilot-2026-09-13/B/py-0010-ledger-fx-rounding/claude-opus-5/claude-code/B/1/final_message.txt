**Cause:** `ledger/fx.py:convert` used `int(cents * rate)`, which truncates toward zero rather than rounding. Every FX-converted leg lost its sub-cent remainder. Account 4471 (EU Receivables) is the only non-USD account, so it's the only one where conversion actually happens — 455 USD legs, and because truncation always pulls toward zero, the bias on the debit legs didn't cancel the bias on the credit legs. Net: 0.30 EUR. All other accounts are USD, converted at rate 1.0, so they were exact.

**Fix:** `convert` now rounds to the nearest minor unit with ties away from zero, using `Decimal` so the rate is interpreted as written (0.9228, not its float approximation) and negative amounts round symmetrically. Same-currency conversions short-circuit to an exact passthrough.
