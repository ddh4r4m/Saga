**Cause:** `ledger/fx.py:convert` used `int(cents * rate)`, which truncates toward zero. **Fix:** `convert` now computes the product with `Decimal` (from `str(rate)`, so the rate isn't polluted by binary float artifacts) and quantizes to the nearest minor unit, ties to even.
