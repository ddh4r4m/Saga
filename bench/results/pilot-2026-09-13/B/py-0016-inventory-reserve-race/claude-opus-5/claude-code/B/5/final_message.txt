The oversell was a lost-update race: `reserve` read stock, then wrote it back with no atomicity between the two store calls, so two threads both read 5 and both wrote 2.

Fix, confined to `inventory/reserve.py` (`store.py` and `tests/**` are OUT of scope per the contract):

- `Inventory` now holds a per-SKU `threading.RLock`, created lazily under a small guard lock (`inventory/reserve.py:22`).
- `reserve`, `release`, and `available` each hold that SKU's lock across the whole get/set pair, making the read-modify-write atomic. Per-SKU rather than one global lock so slow (network-backed) reads for unrelated SKUs don't serialize.
- Public signatures are untouched; the argument validation still happens before any locking.
